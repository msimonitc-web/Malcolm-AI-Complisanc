# Complisey Academy — Product Roadmap & Strategic Directives

## Upcoming Course Offerings & Expansions (To Be Implemented After Testing)

1. **Annual Refresher Modules (30–45 mins)**
   - Concise annual statutory updates reflecting new Seychelles Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) circulars, guidance notes, and National Risk Assessment (NRA) findings.
   - Designed for rapid annual recertification and statutory audit readiness under Section 34 of the AML/CFT Act 2020.

2. **Role-Specific Mini-Tracks (45 mins)**
   - **Board of Directors & Senior Management Track**:
     - Focus on statutory governance liabilities, personal and institutional liabilities under Section 52, supervisory oversight, and MLRO appointment mandates.
   - **Frontline & Client-Facing Staff Track**:
     - Focus on day-to-day CDD collection, rapid red-flag detection, safe customer handling, and strict Section 48 anti-tipping-off adherence.
